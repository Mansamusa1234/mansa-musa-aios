Our Story

Named after the richest king who ever lived — and just as generous.

Mansa Musa serves authentic Caribbean food the way it should be: marinated overnight, slow-cooked, and packed with flavour. From smoky jerk off the grill to rich, tender oxtail, every box leaves our Poplar Road kitchen fresh and full. Real ingredients, real spice, real Caribbean.
